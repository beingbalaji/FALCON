Model details and diagnostic checks

Model: DeBERTa v3 xsmall (ONNX), pretrained on SNLI and MultiNLI. A strong source relationship must score at least 0.90; competing support and contradiction produce a conflict result. These thresholds have not been calibrated for general fact-checking.

Run nine fixed, hand-written sentence-pair checks to inspect model behavior. These are not a representative accuracy benchmark or training data.

THE REVIEW PROCESS

Three questions to ask

  1. 01
    Who published it?

    Look for a named author, a source, and the original date.

  2. 02
    What evidence is shown?

    Find the primary study, statement, document, or data behind the claim.

  3. 03
    Who else confirms it?

    Check independent reporting and corrections before sharing.

A polished writing style is not proof. An emotional style is not proof of falsehood.

    03 / UNDERSTAND

    What the report means

    FALCON looks for transparent language cues: sensational phrasing, requests to share urgently, sweeping claims, vague authority, and emphatic formatting. It reports matches without assigning a truth score or estimating the probability that a claim is false.

    Topic detection uses a short keyword list. The dashboard groups only items you analyzed in this browser. A neutral style does not make a claim true, and sensational style does not make it false. Check current primary sources and independent reporting. The evidence tool above uses a separate pretrained DeBERTa model and retrieved passages. This writing-cue report cannot establish factual accuracy. The original research proposal’s BERT training, live social streams, and federated learning remain unimplemented.
