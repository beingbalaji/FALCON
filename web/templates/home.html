How it works

  1. Records. Capitals, leaders, authors and dates are compared with Wikidata entries directly.
  2. Search. Other claims are searched in Wikipedia, and every sentence is a candidate.
  3. Rank. A neural reranker keeps the passages that actually address the claim.
  4. Weigh. A DeBERTa model trained on FEVER decides whether each passage supports or contradicts it.
  5. Abstain. When the evidence is weak, FALXON says so instead of guessing.
